# Security policy

ClauseCheck holds contracts that people are often under a duty to keep confidential, so a weakness here is serious. If you find one, please tell us.

## Reporting

Report privately through GitHub: **[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/hapsarilw/contract-checker/security/advisories/new)**. Only the maintainers can see the report. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think an attacker could do with it.

## What to expect

- **We acknowledge every report within 72 hours** (NFR-2.13).
- We keep you informed while we fix it, and agree a disclosure date with you. We aim to fix the most serious problems first and fastest.
- We credit you when we disclose, if you want to be credited.

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue or support legal action against anyone who researches in good faith within these rules:

- Use only accounts you created yourself. Never access, change or delete another person's data. If you reach someone else's data by accident, stop, do not keep it, and tell us.
- Do not degrade the service for others: no denial-of-service testing, no load testing, no spam.
- Do not use social engineering or physical attacks, and do not attack our providers.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before you disclose it.

## Scope

The ClauseCheck web application and API, and this repository. Our providers' own services (the AI provider, hosting, database and email) are out of scope; report problems in them to those providers.

The machine-readable contact is published at `/.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116). It expires on 2027-10-05 and must be renewed before then.
